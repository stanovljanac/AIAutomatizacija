The cite-or-refuse prompt
PROMPT · paste at the top of any AI chat
For every factual claim, name the specific real source it comes from (title, author/site, date). If you can't point to a real, checkable source, don't make the claim — say "I couldn't verify this." Never invent citations, quotes, case names, numbers, or links.
