## English terminology consistency (the small replacement)

Keep terms consistent across videos so the channel feels authored, not assembled:

- Prefer plain words over jargon; define a term on first use (STYLE_GUIDE §2).
- Pick one spelling/casing per tool and stick to it: **Google Sheets, Excel, Outlook,
  Notion, Airtable, Apps Script, Office Scripts, Power Automate, Claude, ChatGPT,
  Codex, Make, Zapier, n8n.**
- "automation" (the thing) vs "to automate" (the verb) — don't mix into "automatization".
- "spreadsheet" not "sheet" when referring to the file; "a Sheet/tab" for a single tab.
- "workflow", "trigger", "script", "formula", "macro" — use precisely, not as filler.
- Numbers spoken naturally in narration ("about a hundred rows").

When unsure, the script-review skill flags inconsistent term usage.
