# The spreadsheet — rows, green checks, and the ONE gold flag

**Purpose:** record how the channel draws the ledger the work lands in (7 scenes) — the destination
of nearly every automation we show — and the "one row, not a hundred" payoff it exists to set up.
**When to read:** read this before any beat where data lands in a sheet/table, rows are filled,
checked, flagged, or reviewed.
**Do not duplicate:** if writing about sheets, rows, cells, or tables, extend this file; the thing
that *becomes* a row is [source-document](source-document.md); the human checking it is
[human-gate](human-gate.md).

## The subject

The channel's "after" state, and its most literal proof surface. The spreadsheet is where an
abstract claim ("it works") becomes countable: 40/40, 6/40, one flagged row out of a hundred.
It is also the **payoff shape** the channel argues for — *the human checks one row, not a hundred*
([only-flags](../../../templates/hyperframes/scenes/only-flags/index.html), 008 s09).

## What landed (owner verdicts)

- **The same sheet, twice, with the same clock.** 014 s2 (by hand, 6/40, clock crawling to 54) and
  s3 (one pass, 40/40, clock **snaps 54 → 0**) reuse identical data so the speed contrast is
  explicit and *earned*. This is the library's cleanest proof structure — copy the structure, not the scene.
- **One gold flag beats a wall of green.** `only-flags`' whole thesis is the ratio: the value is
  not "it filled the sheet", it's "you now look at **one** row".
- **Show the arithmetic** (`receipts-mathcheck`, 008's thesis): the panel re-adds subtotal + tax and
  lets the viewer watch the totals agree. This scene exists **because the storyboard had flattened
  it into a plain text card — the slideshow the owner rejected.**
- **Vocabulary continuity across a video:** 008's body scenes share one surface, one green ✓ / gold
  REVIEW language, and even **the same flagged vendor** ("Marlow Street Market") in
  `only-flags` and `receipts-mathcheck`.

## What was rejected (owner verdicts — dated, never the agent's own opinion)

- **The flattened card.** `receipts-mathcheck`, `only-flags`, `monthly-retype`, and
  `limits-privacy` were each **restored from the script's bespoke intent after the storyboard
  flattened them into cards** — the slideshow the owner rejected. This is the single most repeated
  failure in the library: a sheet beat is *not* a bullet list about a sheet. See the
  [title-card lesson](../lessons/2026-06-28-title-card-scenes-get-rejected.md).
- **Real client data.** Demo data is **synthetic, always** — fake names, items, addresses,
  vendors (`wh-slow-machine`, `wh-one-pass` both state it explicitly).
- **3D-tilted sheets** — flat and face-on, mobile-first
  ([lesson](../lessons/2026-07-12-no-3d-tilt-on-content-cards.md)).

## Recurring elements

- **A counter over the rows** — `6/40`, `0→40`, `40/40 — one pass`. Racing or **sticking**; both
  are arguments.
- **A clock** conveying tedium (`~10 min`, crawling to 54) — and, if the automation wins, the
  **same clock snapping to 0**.
- **A cursor with intent** — placing fields one at a time (chore), or **pulling back and going
  still** (the automation working). Cursor stillness is the channel's "I just watched" beat.
- **A per-row check landing in cascade**, never all at once — the cascade *is* the proof.
- **One anomaly**: `??.??`, a red total, a gold NOT SURE / REVIEW flag. The sheet always has exactly
  one thing wrong with it.

## Colors & proportions

- **Blue surface** (body palette) · **green `#22D3A7` ✓ = row passed** · **gold `#FFB020` REVIEW =
  a human should look** · **red `#ff5c5c` = wrong**. Green is per-row and cheap; gold is scarce and
  means judgment ([verdict-stamp](verdict-stamp.md)).
- **Flat, face-on, filling the stage**, above the 19.5% caption band. Cell text must be
  **mobile-legible** — this is the constraint that kills realistic sheet density: draw ~5–8 rows
  with big type, never a screenshot-accurate grid.
- Portrait: fewer rows, bigger cells. Never shrink to fit more.

## Avoid

- **A realistic, dense spreadsheet** — unreadable on a phone and instantly boring.
- **A sheet that fills with nothing wrong** — no anomaly, no beat.
- **All-green as the payoff.** All-green is [the lie](failure.md); the payoff is the **one flag**.
- **Narrating the count** instead of showing the counter
  ([lesson](../lessons/2026-07-07-dont-break-momentum-with-a-spoken-stat.md)).
- **A bullet list describing the sheet** — the standing reject above.
