# Title-card scenes get rejected — every scene must visualize its subject

**Purpose:** this file exists to explain why title-on-background scenes are a hard
reject and what replaced them.
**When to read:** read this when planning or reviewing scene visuals, or when tempted
to ship a scene that is a title/subtitle over a styled background.
**Do not duplicate:** if writing about title-card rejections or bespoke-scene
standards, extend this file instead of creating a new one.

## Finding

Scenes that are only a title/subtitle over a background (however styled) get rejected
by the owner every time; a scene passes only when it **visualizes its subject with
concrete motion**.

## Evidence

- **2026-06-24 — video 008 render rejected** as "another template slideshow": the
  fixed-template gallery produced the same deck with new words
  ([PROGRESS 2026-06-24](../../../docs/PROGRESS.md)).
- **2026-06-28 — video 009 Short first render rejected**: of 8 scenes the owner kept
  only S4 (`MoneyLeakRun` — a spreadsheet that visibly RUNS); every other scene was
  "empty slides with just titles". The rebuild to 7/7 bespoke scenes
  (`bespoke_ratio` 100% vs the rejected 4/8) passed
  ([PROGRESS 2026-06-28](../../../docs/PROGRESS.md)).
- **2026-06-29 — the pattern repeated at finer grain**: the owner rejected 009's S1
  opener again for a *static foreground* over a dynamic background — replaced by the
  `HookFeedHype` doom-scroll scene that mirrors the viewer's feed.

## Decision

Every non-CTA scene is conceived like a strategist and must visualize its subject —
the standard lives canonically in [MOTION_SPEC §0](../../../style/MOTION_SPEC.md)
(reference scene: 009 S1 `HookFeedHype`; 6-point checklist). Enforced **in code**: the
hard QA check `no_title_card` in
[check-lib.mjs](../../../pipeline/05-qa/lib/check-lib.mjs) fails title-only scene
kinds. This lesson is the evidence trail behind that rule (maturation per
[lifecycle](../../bootstrap/specs/lifecycle.md) §4: rejection → lesson → spec rule →
QA gate).
