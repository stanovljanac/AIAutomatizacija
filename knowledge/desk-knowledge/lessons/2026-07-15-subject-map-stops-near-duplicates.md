# A near-duplicate hides when ideas have no coordinate — give each a subject, gate the seed

**Purpose:** explain why every idea now carries a `subject` coordinate and why a brief can't be
scripted without an idea-pass verdict — the two guards that stop a repeat of the 015 miss.
**When to read:** read this when picking/scoring a topic, adding a free-text seed to a
repeatable-format idea, or wondering whether a new idea is a real cluster-mate or a re-tread.
**Do not duplicate:** if writing about the subject taxonomy or the idea-guard, extend this file
and `docs/CHANNEL_MAP.md` instead of creating a new note.

## Finding

Two independent holes let **015** ("emails I shouldn't answer") reach the script stage as a
near-duplicate of the already-shipped **011** (same subject: inbox; same mechanic: read a pile →
sort → surface the few that matter; same "you still decide" beat):

1. **No map of the territory.** Ideas were tagged by task/sector/tool/lane but had **no subject
   coordinate**, so two videos about the same underlying decision (*what deserves my attention?*)
   had no field in common to collide on. The picker's variety soft-cap (`extendsRun`) guards
   lane/archetype/tool — never subject — so the collision was invisible.
2. **A seed that skipped the idea-pass.** 015 lived as free text inside the `everyone-asks-ai-series`
   *format* idea and was consumed straight into a script, inheriting the format's `value_band`
   instead of being scored on its own merits. The idea-pass (`rubric.mjs`) never ran on *this* idea.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-15 — 015 retired (D-059).** Owner judged the drafted 015 too weak to ship: a
  near-duplicate of 011. Root cause named in the decision: "there is no map of the territory — no
  coordinate per idea, so collisions can't be seen." 015 was replaced by **017** ("The AI Agent"
  Ep.1, a *different* subject: change-detection/policy).

## Decision

The smallest guard that stops the next 015 (two mechanisms + a map), D-059:

- **Subject taxonomy.** Every idea gets `subject: "branch/leaf"` under *AI Decision Automation*
  (attention · change-detection · failure-detection · execution). Human map in
  [CHANNEL_MAP.md](../../../docs/CHANNEL_MAP.md); machine mirror in
  `pipeline/00-ideas/produced_subjects.json`. 011 & 016 → `attention/inbox`; 017 →
  `change-detection/policy`.
- **Subject-collision warning** in [pick-next.mjs](../../../pipeline/00-ideas/pick-next.mjs)
  (`subjectCollision`, surfaced by `--dry-run`): a ⚠ at pick time when a candidate's subject is
  already claimed. **Never blocks** — a deliberate cluster-mate (016 next to 011) is good; the
  warning just forces the "is this a *distinct* angle?" check.
- **Seed-gate** ([seed-gate.mjs](../../../pipeline/00-ideas/seed-gate.mjs)): a brief may not be
  scripted unless it carries an idea-pass `value_band` (`qualify`/`owner`, not `rejected`). Wired
  as **Step 0.0** of the script-writing skill. Note: the seed-gate alone would *not* have caught
  015 (it inherited a `qualify` band) — the subject guard is the one that catches near-duplicates;
  the seed-gate closes the *unscored*-seed hole.
