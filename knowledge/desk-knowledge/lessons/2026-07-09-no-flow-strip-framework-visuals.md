# Frameworks drawn as three-icons-in-a-row get rejected — give concepts full-stage treatment

**Purpose:** this file exists to explain why concept/framework visuals (e.g.
Draft → Check → Gate) may never ship as a horizontal icon-arrow strip.
**When to read:** read this when storyboarding or authoring any scene that visualizes a
multi-step concept, loop, or framework.
**Do not duplicate:** if writing about framework/concept scene visuals, extend this file
instead of creating a new one.

## Finding

Visualizing a recurring framework as a horizontal strip of small icons with arrows
("draft → check → gate" as three icons in a row) reads as filler and got explicitly
rejected by the owner — even when the underlying concept is good enough to repeat across
videos, the *strip* presentation is what kills it.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-09, 012 Gate ②:** owner, verbatim intent — "do sada su bili uglavnom draft
  check kapija sa 3 glupe ikonice horizontalno jedna do druge sa malom strelicom ka
  sledećem… treba mi nešto novo, veće, dinamično." The concept may stay (and may become a
  series signature) **only if** the visuals earn it; placement moved to the video's end
  pending the visual bar ([012 log](../../../content/012-studio-reveal/log.md)).
- Same failure family as [title-card scenes get rejected](2026-06-28-title-card-scenes-get-rejected.md):
  a graphic that merely *displays* the words instead of landing the idea.

## Decision

Banned pattern added to `style/MOTION_SPEC.md` §6: no three-icons-in-a-row flow strip for
any concept/framework. Frameworks get **full-stage acts** (each step its own full-frame
word-world in its own medium), **territory fields** (large labeled zones on a diagram), or
**morphs** (one shape re-skinned across contexts) — big, new, dynamic; vertical stacking
over horizontal strips. First application: 012 s14 (DRAFT/CHECK/GATE territories) and s16
(three-act word-worlds → vertical monolith).
