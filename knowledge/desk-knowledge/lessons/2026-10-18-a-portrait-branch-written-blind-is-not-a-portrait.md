# A portrait branch written blind is not a portrait — look at the tall frame before a Short reaches the owner

**Purpose:** this file exists so no Short cut from a long reaches the owner on the strength of a
portrait branch nobody has looked at, and so "it has a `.portrait` rule" is never again read as "it
works in 9:16".
**When to read:** read before authoring a scene's portrait branch, before deriving Shorts with
`make-shorts.mjs`, and before signing off `props.portrait_ok`.
**Do not duplicate:** the cutting mechanics live in the 2026-10-17 lesson and D-074; the sizing
numbers in MOTION_SPEC §10; the layering rule in MOTION_SPEC §13. Extend this file with new
portrait failures.


## Finding

1. **The first two Shorts cut from a long were rejected outright.** 023's "3 Rules to Make an AI
   Actually Focus" (s21-s24) and "Pasting More Into an AI Costs You Twice" (s16-s19). Owner,
   2026-10-18: *"katastrofalni … dosta stvari se preklapa vizuelno jedno sa drugim i nije jasno šta se
   radi dok su takođe i kontrasti loši"* — elements overlap each other, it is unclear what is
   happening, contrast is poor. The content also sat in the top ~55% of the frame.
2. **No scene was new — the framings were.** Every reused scene's `.portrait` branch was written on
   2026-08-23 with the 023 scene library and never rendered tall or looked at, because 023's own Short
   used separate `ats-*` scenes. The only change made at cut time was `at3d-pairs`' portrait camera
   (reverted). So the defect was old, unreviewed work — and the system waved it through.
3. **Every check passed it.** `portraitReady` counted any `.portrait` rule as ready; the frame gate
   measures detail and coverage, and sees neither overlap nor contrast; script review reads words. D-054
   had already asked for overlap checks "in both orientations at authoring time" — in prose only, so
   nothing ran it.
4. **The old longs are below today's floor anyway.** Even a perfect 9:16 framing of 022-025 reuses
   visuals the owner rates far weaker than the 025 benchmark (visually, not semantically).

## Evidence

2026-10-18: owner deleted `content/023-attention/short-2/` and `short-3/` (65 s and 63 s, both through
check.mjs, the frame gate and a second-pass script review); `git log` dates all seven
reused scenes' portrait CSS to commit 4f29d6e (2026-08-23).

## Decision

- **No Shorts cut from 022-025.** 1B starts with the next long, whose scenes are authored with both
  framings from the start.
- **A portrait framing is ready only after its tall frame was looked at** at phone size: nothing lands
  over anything (MOTION_SPEC §13), the subject and every word read against the background (contrast),
  the subject uses the frame instead of its top half (fill, §10). The sign-off is
  `props.portrait_ok: true`; `05-qa/check.mjs` refuses a derived Short with any reused landscape scene
  unsigned (HARD, `portrait_signoff`), and `make-shorts.mjs` lists the unsigned scenes up front.
- **The same look happens when the branch is written**, not first at cut time (storyboard skill).
- **Open:** an automatic overlap/contrast measure for the frame gate — ink coverage cannot see either,
  so today the sign-off rests on looking.
