# When the owner authors the visuals, invert the sync — fit narration to the animation, don't regenerate it

**Purpose:** this file exists to record the pipeline inversion 018 needed — the master is the
owner's hand-recorded animation, so voice/alignment bends to the picture, not the reverse.
**When to read:** read this when a video's visuals are **authored by the owner** (a recorded
GSAP/After-Effects animation, a hand-drawn film) rather than generated from the scene-plan —
i.e. any pinned brand-film or personal-story piece.
**Do not duplicate:** the normal audio-drives-visuals contract lives in
[voice-synthesis](../../../.claude/skills/voice-synthesis/SKILL.md) and
[ARCHITECTURE](../../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md); this is the documented exception, not a rewrite.

## Finding

The pipeline's core contract is **audio drives visuals** — one continuous narration track is
synthesized first, then scenes snap to the forced-alignment timestamps (so a scene change never
cuts the voice). For a video whose **visuals are the owner's own recorded animation** that
contract inverts: the animation is a fixed master with real scene cuts at fixed seconds, and the
job becomes making the narration **fit the picture**. You do **not** re-record or re-pace the
animation to the voice — you (a) trim/rewrite lines so the assembled track fits the animation's
duration, then (b) **re-snap each line's start onto the animation's real scene cuts** via a
per-scene affine mapping (`mp4_time ≈ a·source_time + b`), so no line crosses a cut. Re-aligning
kept-audio onto a re-recorded animation is far cheaper than re-recording the voice.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-18 — video 018 (pinned life-story brand-film)**: v3 was voice-led (assembled track
  ~104.3 s, animation to be paced to it). The owner instead **recorded the animation** (`mystory.mp4`,
  ~100 s), so we flipped to "Option B": trim the **voice** to fit — four approved script edits +
  tighter micro-pauses took the v4 track to **98.66 s**, and the lines were then snapped to the
  recording's real scene cuts (`content/018-life-story/narration-final.md`,
  `narration-timing-v4.json`).
- **2026-07-18 (v6)** — the owner re-recorded the animation again (`story.mp4`, 99.78 s, three HTML
  timing edits); the **same** v4 line audio was re-aligned onto it scene-driven via an affine map
  (`mp4 = 0.785·gsap + 1.66`) → `preview-narration-v6.mp4`. Overruns all shrank vs v5. **Open
  issue:** line 13 ("Then one day, I stopped") still lands ~4 s late (S4 VO overflow) — owner judges
  on the preview, or trims S4. Reusable scratchpad toolchain: `extract`/`calibrate`/`build`/
  `reassemble-v6.mjs` + `montage.mjs`.

## Decision

For an owner-authored-visuals video, treat the animation as the immovable master: **fit narration
to it** (trim/rewrite lines to its duration, then affine-snap line starts to its scene cuts) rather
than regenerating the picture. Prefer **re-aligning kept audio** over re-recording when the owner
re-cuts the animation. This is a **deliberate exception** to the audio-drives-visuals contract,
scoped to authored-visual pieces — the default pipeline is unchanged. Single application (018), with
one unresolved per-line overrun still open → stays `draft`; graduate when a second authored-visual
video confirms the workflow (or the affine re-snap toolchain is promoted out of the scratchpad).
