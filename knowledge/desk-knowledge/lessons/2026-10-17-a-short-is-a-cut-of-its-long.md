# A Short is a cut of its long — reuse the scenes, re-voice the words, and pay for it in portrait framing

**Purpose:** this file exists so the next Short is cut from the long it belongs to instead of being
produced a second time, and so the three traps of cutting (back-references, broken carries, missing
portrait framing) are known before the render shows them.
**When to read:** read before authoring a long's `short_cuts`, before building a new HyperFrames scene,
and before hand-building any Short.
**Do not duplicate:** the mechanics live in D-074, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §12 (quadrant 2) and the
script-writing / storyboard skills; extend this file with new cutting traps.


## Finding

1. **Every Short so far was a second production.** 022-025 each got 5-7 bespoke portrait scenes
   (`cws-*`, `ats-*`, `fzs-*`, `mvs-*`) telling the long's story again — while 95% of the channel's views
   come from Shorts and the calendar has 12 Short slots a month. The long's scenes already sync to their
   sentences through beats and marks, so the same sentences re-voiced drive the same scene.
2. **A verbatim cut inherits the long's back-references.** 023's strongest standalone runs open on
   "So what do you actually do with this?", "Then something turned up…", "There is one more bill attached
   to all this". Replacing ONE first sentence with ONE sentence keeps the scene's sentence count, so its
   beats still land — any other edit to the words breaks the beat indices and the word warp.
3. **A carry or match out of a scene was composed for the long's next scene.** In a cut it holds only
   when the Short plays that same next scene; otherwise it is a jump with a mismatched last frame.
4. **Reuse costs portrait framing, scene by scene.** 022/023's scenes carry a `.portrait` branch;
   024/025's 3D kits (`hf-world`, `hf-mech`) were authored 16:9 only, which is exactly why their
   Shorts were forked into new scenes. *(2026-10-18: "cut for free" was wrong — the two 023 cuts were
   rejected, the branches had never been looked at. See
   [the follow-up](2026-10-18-a-portrait-branch-written-blind-is-not-a-portrait.md).)*

## Evidence

2026-10-17: two 023 cuts derived with zero new scenes (s21-s24, 59.2 s voiced; s16-s19, 60.8 s), word
counts matched the long scene for scene; a 025 dry run named `mv-twins` and `mv-card` as having no
portrait framing and carried 7 SFX cues across.

## Decision

- Shorts are `short_cuts` in the long's script; `make-shorts.mjs` builds them, `--marks` re-times them.
- Write each long segment so its first sentence stands alone; use `opening` only when it does not.
- Every new scene ships both framings (MOTION_SPEC §10); compile-hyperframes refuses a Short scene
  without one, and 05-qa refuses a cut whose reused scenes were never signed off tall (`portrait_ok`).
- Cut only from the next long on — 022-025 are skipped (owner, 2026-10-18).
- Hand-build a Short scene only for a hook no long scene can open with.
