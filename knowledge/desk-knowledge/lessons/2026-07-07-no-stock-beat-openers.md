# No stock beat-openers — never announce honesty, vary recurring beats per video

**Purpose:** this file exists to record why "Now the honest part." was banned and how
recurring structural beats should open instead.
**When to read:** read this when writing or reviewing a script's honest-catch beat, or
whenever a phrase from a previous video is about to be reused as a beat opener.
**Do not duplicate:** the writing rule lives in
[STYLE_GUIDE §9](../../../style/STYLE_GUIDE.md) (blacklist) and the script-writing skill
(rule 1c) — this is the evidence trail.

## Finding

A recurring structural beat (the honest-catch) had hardened into a **stock spoken opener**
— "Now the honest part." appeared verbatim across videos (009 S6, 011 S6 draft) — and the
owner flagged it: it does nothing, and **announcing** honesty ("to be honest…") is telling
instead of showing. The beat itself is valuable; the announcement is the failure.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-07 — video 011 final-gate review**: owner — "I dont like now the honest part.
  Its doing nothing for me and i dont like how it sounds still i have it in a lot of the
  videos. We should change that in the script and rules as well." Chose (from offered
  options): open **directly on the strongest limitation** ("It won't ship you a startup…");
  keep "Unsexy — that's the point." as the deliberate `everyone-asks-ai` **series
  signature** (that lane only) ([PROGRESS 2026-07-07](../../../docs/PROGRESS.md)).

## Decision

Honest-catch beats open on the limitation itself — no meta-announcement; phrasing varies
per video; no stock beat-opener repeats across videos. Announced-honesty phrases are
blacklisted in [STYLE_GUIDE §9](../../../style/STYLE_GUIDE.md); script-writing rule 1c
carries it at authoring time. Deliberate series signatures are the one exception, and each
must be an explicit owner decision (current: "Unsexy — that's the point.",
`everyone-asks-ai` only). Single data point → `draft`.
