# A re-voice moves every word — a typed word offset is stale the moment the final voice lands

**Purpose:** this file exists so a scene's word-timed hits and the SFX stay on their words when the
draft voice is replaced by the final one.
**When to read:** read before typing any word time into a scene or a plan (an impact, a stamp, a
sound cue), and before re-voicing anything after its scenes were built (since D-076 there is no planned
Azure re-voice, but a script edit after the voice is still one).
**Do not duplicate:** the voice pipeline lives in the voice-synthesis skill, the render order in the
video-render skill; extend this file for any further finding about timing that survives a re-voice.


## Finding

Scene WINDOWS re-time themselves after a re-voice (they come from the alignment), but anything typed as
a number does not. On 024 the Azure final spoke the same 383 words at the draft's pace overall (128.9s vs
127.9s) and every sentence started within ±0.6s of the draft — yet the word offsets INSIDE sentences
moved by up to 0.37s, and the plan's absolute SFX seconds by up to 1.09s by the end. The long cut's nine
scenes carried 76 word marks typed off the draft alignment (`tWrong = b1 + 0.78`); kept as typed, the
impact on "million" would have landed 0.37s late and "every" 0.25s early. The Short did not have the
problem at all: its plan reads every cue off the alignment, so the re-voice was a re-run.

## Evidence

`content/024-frozen/_draft-edge/alignment.json` (edge-tts) vs `content/024-frozen/alignment.json` (Azure,
re-aligned by faster-whisper), 2026-10-09: per-mark drift measured for all 76 marks (0.00–0.37s, 34 over
0.12s); sentence-start drift ±0.6s, 0.84s on the last line. The fix — each mark wrapped as
`off("Name", typed)` in the `fz-*` scenes (absent `props.offsets`, the typed value) and a piecewise-linear
warp on the word starts (draft word i → final word i) in `render/plan.mjs` — put "nothing", "moved",
"frozen", "Ankara" and "instruction" back on their words in the final cut (checked on frames).

## Rule

- Never type a word time as a constant. A scene reads its word marks from props (`cues` / `offsets`),
  and the per-video plan derives them from `alignment.json` — then a re-voice is a re-run, as the
  024 Short's `plan-short.mjs` proved.
- If a scene already has typed marks, carry them across with the word warp (draft alignment → final
  alignment), and move the plan's absolute SFX seconds through the same warp.
- Keep the draft alignment (`_draft-edge/`) until the final ships: it is the reference the typed numbers
  were measured against.
