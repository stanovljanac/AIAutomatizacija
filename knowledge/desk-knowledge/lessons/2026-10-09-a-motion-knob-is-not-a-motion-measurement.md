# A motion knob is not a motion measurement — "10% less motion" is a measured ratio

**Purpose:** this file exists so an owner's "less motion, by N%" is delivered and reported as a
MEASURED change on screen, not as a scale factor that only sounds like one.
**When to read:** read before tuning `HW.motion` (`props.motion.scale` / `focus`), before promising a
percentage of calmer/busier motion, and before reporting how much a cut moves.
**Do not duplicate:** the knob's mechanics live in `style/MOTION_SPEC.md` §16; extend this file for any
further finding about measuring on-screen motion.


## Finding

The 024 Short shipped a motion budget of `scale 0.9` (camera travel and the ambient clock — dust,
twinkle, idle bobbing — at 90%). Measured as motion energy (mean absolute luma change between
consecutive frames), rendering every scene with and without that budget changed the total by only
**−2.9%**; outside the key-take windows two scenes even measured slightly HIGHER. Yet the Short as a
whole moves **26% less** than the long cut. The difference was authoring: the busiest devices were cut,
the pace is 10% slower (the same events spread over more time), and the takes are held still.

## Evidence

`content/024-frozen/short/motion.json` (2026-10-09), made by `content/024-frozen/render/motion-ab.mjs`:
A/B ratio 0.971 overall, 0.684-0.906 inside focus windows; vs the long cut 0.738 overall (s1 0.80,
s2 0.79, s3 0.96, s4 0.66, s5 0.62, s6 0.77). The busiest second of the first cut (45.9) was a macro
pull-back at 2.7→6.5 units from the tiles; s3's remaining peak is a whole-block colour change, which the
metric counts as motion.

## Rule

- State a motion target as a measured ratio against a reference cut ("energy ≈ 0.90 of the long"),
  and check it with `motion-ab.mjs`; report the measurement, never the knob value.
- Camera and ambient are a small share of on-screen change. To calm a scene, remove or slow the STORY
  motion that is not the point of the beat; a slower camera also blurs less, which gives some back.
- Close to a textured surface, camera moves dominate everything — a macro dolly is the first thing to
  shorten.
- The metric cannot tell motion from a lighting/colour change; read the per-half-second curve before
  "fixing" a peak.
