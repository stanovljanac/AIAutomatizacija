# A search term is a guess until YouTube autocomplete says people type it

**Purpose:** this file exists so that the phrase a video is built and titled around is a search people
actually make — not one that merely sounds like it.
**When to read:** read when writing or re-wording an idea's `search_term`, when a seed-gate STOP says
"nobody types …", and before titling a video around a keyword.
**Do not duplicate:** the check and the gate live in `pipeline/00-ideas/search-demand.mjs` and
`seed-gate.mjs` (script-writing Step 0.0); extend this file with what we learn about phrasing.


## Finding

1. **Most of our phrases were not searches.** Of 60 backlog/in-progress ideas, YouTube autocomplete
   offered the phrase (or longer searches starting with it) for **14**; for 46 it offered something
   else. The channel's search traffic agreed: 74 views from search in five months, on terms like
   "ai employee" (3).
2. **The miss is usually small and fixable — the wording, not the topic.** "spreadsheet to pdf invoice"
   → people type "excel to pdf invoice"; "automatically email invoices google sheets" → "automatically
   send email from google sheets"; "clean messy data excel automatically" → "clean messy data in
   excel"; "best ai model for automation" → "best ai for automation".
3. **Concrete "do X in tool Y" phrases verify; abstract or invented ones don't.** Verified: "extract
   data from pdf to excel" (6 longer searches), "find duplicate files" (7), "bulk rename files" (5),
   "data validation google sheets" (3). Not typed: "morning automation routine", "automation stopped
   working", "ai monitor terms of service changes".

## Evidence

`node pipeline/00-ideas/search-demand.mjs`, 2026-10-17 (en/US, `suggestqueries.google.com`,
`ds=yt`); every result is stored on its idea as `search_demand` in `pipeline/00-ideas/ideas.json`,
alternatives included. Channel search numbers: YouTube Analytics `insightTrafficSourceType` +
`insightTrafficSourceDetail`, read-only, same day.

## Decision

- Every idea carries `search_demand`; the seed gate refuses to script an unverified phrase (owner
  waiver with a reason for breaking news or Studio → Research evidence).
- Re-wording is a Gate-1 judgement, never automatic: pick the alternative that keeps the topic, or
  re-angle the idea. Prefer the concrete "do X" search; lead the title with it.
