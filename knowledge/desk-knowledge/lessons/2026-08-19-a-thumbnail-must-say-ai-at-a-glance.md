# A frame from our own video is rarely a thumbnail — it must say "AI" and name the mechanism without narration

**Purpose:** so we stop spending a render-day extracting candidate stills for explainer videos that
the owner will reject, and so every thumbnail concept is judged by the one test that decides clicks.
**When to read:** at the thumbnail step of any video (Step 3.2), before running
`pipeline/04b-thumbnails/extract.mjs`, and before writing a thumbnail prompt.
**Do not duplicate:** the procedure lives in the
[visual-prompts skill](../../../.claude/skills/visual-prompts/SKILL.md) — extend that for *how*,
this file for *why*.

## Finding

**A scene still works inside the video because the narration is carrying it. On the thumbnail there
is no narration.** Our explainer scenes are deliberately abstract (a pile, a sheet, a column, a
monolith), so a frame lifted out of one shows an object with no subject: a stranger scrolling sees
paper, not AI, and not "context window".

The extractor scores exactly the properties that *don't* decide this — one large focal object, high
contrast, minimal text. 022's rejected candidates scored **110** on that scale. The score is a
legibility floor, not a click predictor, and it should never be read as "this one is good enough".

What the shipped image actually needed, all three at once:

1. **A subject signifier** — something unmistakably AI in the frame (a chat panel, a machine head).
   Without it the picture is about paper.
2. **The mechanism, not the topic** — the thing the video reframes, drawn: a chat panel with four
   short bubbles pouring one endless receipt into a mound far bigger than the bubbles could hold.
3. **A headline zone reserved in the render, and the SEARCH PHRASE in it** — the shipped headline is
   *"WHAT IS A CONTEXT WINDOW"* (the query), not the thesis line *"IT NEVER REMEMBERED"* we had
   drafted as the overlay phrase. Title and thumbnail both front-load the query; the thesis is the
   payoff inside the video.

For a **Desk Lessons / concept explainer, go to prompts first** and treat extraction as the bonus,
not the primary flow. For a demo or a screen-capture video, where a frame shows a real artefact
doing something, extraction still has a job.

## Evidence

- **022 (context window), 2026-08-16→18:** 7 candidates extracted (3 auto + 4 manual grabs, scores
  75–110). The owner rejected **all 7**, then rejected the first prompt pair too — *"one object that
  has nothing to do with the story; nobody seeing stacked paper thinks AI or context window."*
  The second prompt set added an AI signifier to every option (A chat-with-endless-receipt, B the
  head that re-reads and forgets, C what-you-typed-vs-what-it-read); **A shipped**
  (`content/022-context-window/images/thumb.jpeg`), headline composited in the reserved left third.
- **021 (what is a token), 2026-08-15:** 7 candidates, scores 55–70, `chosen: false` on every one —
  no pick was ever recorded. Across 021 and 022 that is **14 extracted candidates and zero shipped
  thumbnails** for the explainer format.
- Two of 022's candidates (#1, #4) were grabbed from the **v1** hook and survived the v2 re-render in
  `thumb_candidates.json` — frames that were no longer anywhere in the shipped video. Candidates die
  with the cut; re-extract after any re-render.
- `thumb_candidates.json` also carried `source: "manual-grab"`, which is not in the schema enum, so
  `--choose-thumb` would have thrown on the owner's pick: a hand-added candidate must be written
  through the schema, never typed in.

## Decision

- **Explainer/concept video → write the prompts, don't shop the extraction.** Every prompt option
  carries an AI signifier + the mechanism + reserved negative space for the headline, and states its
  `overlay_phrase`. Extraction still runs (it is free and it feeds CTR learning) but its output is
  offered as an alternative, not as the recommendation.
- **The headline on the thumbnail is the search phrase**, matching the keyword-first title; the
  thesis line stays in the video.
- **Re-extract candidates after every re-render**, and drop the ones whose beat is no longer in the
  cut. A candidate that is not in the shipped video is not a candidate.
- The owner still generates the image in a free tool and still makes the pick — no AI reviewer, no
  auto-generation (unchanged, 2026-06-07 / 2026-07-11).
