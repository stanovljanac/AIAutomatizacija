# Fill the stage; transitions must never reflow layout

**Purpose:** this file exists to record why small centered content clusters and
height-collapse transitions get rejected, and what layout contract replaced them.
**When to read:** read this when authoring or reviewing any scene component's layout or
an in-scene visual swap/transition.
**Do not duplicate:** the canonical rule lives in
[MOTION_SPEC §0](../../../style/MOTION_SPEC.md) ("Fill the stage") — this is the evidence
trail behind it.

## Finding

Two distinct visual failures draw the same owner rejection: (a) scene content floating as
a **small cluster in the middle** of the portrait frame (undersized type/icons, empty
bands above and below), and (b) an in-scene transition that **reflows layout** — an
element whose *height* animates away visibly "shifts the page" instead of reading as a
designed motion.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-07 — video 011 final-gate review**: the owner rejected S2 (`AppToInboxFlip`
  v1) and flagged the S7 frame: "It should always take as much space as possible… 5% up
  margin and 15% bottom due to subtitles"; "it just shifts the height… not good"; "make
  all slightly bigger — icons, font size." S2 v1 collapsed the app window via
  height/scaleY (reflow jank); several scenes sat as small centered clusters
  ([PROGRESS 2026-07-07](../../../docs/PROGRESS.md)).
- Fix shipped the same day: content spans the **5% → 85% band** (bottom 15% = captions),
  type/icons scaled up across S2/S3/S5/S6/S7, and S2's swap rebuilt as absolutely-
  positioned layers moved by `transform`/`opacity` (app flies out, inbox slides up — no
  reflow).

## Decision

Every scene component honors the stage contract now written in
[MOTION_SPEC §0](../../../style/MOTION_SPEC.md): content fills the top 5%→85% band with
big type; in-scene swaps use absolutely-positioned layers + `transform`/`opacity` only —
never animate an element's flow height. Single rejection cycle → `draft`; graduate when
the next video's scenes pass the gate under this contract.
