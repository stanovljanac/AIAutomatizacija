# Don't break a reframe's momentum with a spoken stat — demote it to a sourced chip

**Purpose:** this file exists to explain why reciting a supporting statistic mid-narration
hurts a story/reframe Short, and what to do with the number instead.
**When to read:** read this when writing or reviewing a Short (especially the
`everyone-asks-ai` reframe lane), or whenever a scene's job is to carry momentum and you
are tempted to state a percentage or hours-per-day figure aloud.
**Do not duplicate:** if writing about stat placement or on-screen sourcing, extend this
file (and cross-check [on-screen source for stats / D-026](../../../docs/DECISIONS.md)).

## Finding

In a narrative reframe (hook → "boring opposite" → build), pausing to **speak** a
supporting statistic flattens the momentum — it reads like a TED talk and drops the
viewer out of the story. The number still adds credibility, so it belongs on screen as a
small sourced **corner chip**, not in the narration. Carry the beat with **concrete
experience** (show the mess) instead.

## Evidence

- **2026-07-07 — video 011 Short, 2nd-model review (owner-relayed)**: S3 originally spoke
  "the average worker burns about two and a half hours a day on email…" right after the
  strong reframe. The reviewer flagged it as the weakest beat — "prekida momentum… liči na
  TED talk" — while praising the hook, the visible S4 run, and the honest catch. Rewrote S3
  **experience-first** ("214 unread. Newsletters, receipts, payment alerts. And somewhere in
  there — the one client I actually needed") and demoted the ~2.6 hrs/day figure to a sourced
  corner chip ([PROGRESS 2026-07-07](../../../docs/PROGRESS.md)).

## Decision

For narrative Shorts, **open beats on concrete experience, keep supporting stats as sourced
on-screen chips** (never a spoken "the average worker…" line). This does not weaken
accuracy — the figure still shows with its source per D-026 — it protects pacing. The
sole spoken numbers a beat should carry are the ones that ARE the moment (e.g. the live
counter in a [visible run](2026-06-27-proof-must-be-visible.md)), not background context.
Single data point so far → `draft`; graduate to `stable` if the next Short confirms it or
it becomes a script-review check.
