# A term nobody was shown is noise — set the system up, then an everyday parallel before every name

**Purpose:** this file exists so a script never again goes deep on a system the viewer was never
shown, with a stack of our own words (draft, job card, proof line, silent failure) and no everyday
picture under them.
**When to read:** read before outlining a script, and before scoring a script or a cut at review.
**Do not duplicate:** the rule itself is STYLE_GUIDE §2.7 and D-079; the gate text lives in
`pipeline/shared/review/rubric.mjs` (`plain_explained`). Extend this file with new cases of a
newcomer getting lost.


## Finding

1. **The owner's verdict on the last few videos (2026-10-20):** we stamped words and never turned
   to explain, on a basic example, why a thing works the way it does. 026 talked about the email, the
   draft and the silent failure. *"Uđe neko u klip i niko ne zna o čemu se radi ni odakle meni taj
   sistem ni zašto ga koristim ni na koji način ni zašto sam neke stvari odradio."* Someone who walks
   in does not know what is going on, where the system came from, what it is for, or why it was built
   that way. The videos went deep on invented systems that nobody needs.
2. **026 shows the pattern.** s9: *"I deleted the sentence that hid the failure … It was in my own
   job card, in my last video."* The job card is a 025 object this viewer never saw. The renovation
   company's form, the morning report and the PROOF/ALARM lines are all parts of a test setup built for
   the video. They come one after another, and none of them gets an everyday picture first. A smoke
   alarm that only beeps when its battery dies already carries the whole idea of "an alarm that only
   speaks on failure".
3. **Every check passed it.** 026's script review scored 8.2 → 8.3 → 8.6 with every hard gate true.
   The stranger test existed only in the idea pass (is the TOPIC worth it to a stranger), and nothing
   asked whether a stranger could FOLLOW the explanation. Style rules banned engineer jargon such as
   "Pipeline A/B" but not depth without setup.

## Evidence

`content/026-silent-failures/script.review.json` (iterations 1–3, all gates true, 8.6) against the
owner's message of 2026-10-20. The shipped 026 script names the job card, the form, the report and the
two lines without an everyday parallel ahead of any of them; the dead man's handle (s8) is the one
real-world picture, and it arrives at 2:25.

## Decision

- **D-079, STYLE_GUIDE §2.7:** set the system up in plain words before the depth (what it is, what
  it's for, where it came from, why it works this way). Give every idea an everyday parallel or a basic
  worked example before its name. Run examples on things the viewer has. Introduce one new term at a
  time.
- **A fifth script hard gate, `plain_explained`**, so a script that loses a newcomer never reaches the
  owner. The idea-pass `on_brand` gate now asks that the lesson be explainable on something the viewer
  already has.
- **The parallel is planned at the outline**, one per new idea, not patched in after review.
